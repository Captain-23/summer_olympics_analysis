About the Olympic Games

The modern Olympic Games are a major international multi-sport event that brings together athletes from almost every nation in the world. First held in Athens in 1896, the Summer Olympic Games are organized every four years.

Over time, the Olympics have grown to include hundreds of events across dozens of sports, showcasing the highest level of human performance, dedication, and sportsmanship while promoting peace and cultural exchange among nations.
